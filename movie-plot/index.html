Fake It Until We Fall

Character Description

  • Emma is bright, energetic, expressive, and naturally outgoing. She tends to act on her feelings without thinking too much and has a habit of getting overly excited about the things she likes. She is friendly and affectionate, but she can also be stubborn and easily embarrassed when her feelings are hurt.
  • Jack is quiet, reserved, blunt, and easily irritated. He doesn't like unnecessary attention and prefers to keep his thoughts and feelings to himself. He can come across as cold and arrogant because he rarely considers how his words affect other people. Despite his harsh exterior, Jack is observant and secretly considerate. Once he becomes attached to someone, he tends to care about them more than he is willing to admit.
  • Wesley is kind, gentle, polite, and popular among everyone. As the talented basketball team member, he naturally attracts attention but remains humble. He values his deep friendship with Jack and cares for those around him, though he can be a bit late in recognizing his own romantic feelings.
Act 1

Set up

[Action scene]

The final seconds of a high school basketball game. The score is tied. Wesley drives toward the basket while Jack blocks a defender for him. Wesley scores at the buzzer, and the gym explodes. Louder than anyone, Emma jumps up in the stands, waving a homemade banner: "GO WESLEY!!" On the court, Jack glances up at her and frowns.

Jack: "Is she always this loud?"

Wesley (laughing): "She comes to every game."

[Genre / Location / Main characters]

This is a romance set in a high school. Emma is an outgoing, cheerful student who has had a crush on Wesley for a long time. Jack and Wesley have been childhood friends since they were very young, and their families are close too. All three are members of the school basketball team. Whenever Wesley has a game, at school or anywhere else, Emma makes sure to be there cheering him on.

Jack is cold and composed on the outside but warm-hearted deep down. When he first saw Emma, he thought, ‘She's a little annoying.’ Her nonstop, over-the-top cheering at every game bothered him, and he couldn't imagine ever getting along with a girl like her.

[Problem]

Lots of girls like Wesley because he is kind and gentle. Emma believes Wesley might like her back, since she has supported him for so long, but she knows she needs a little push to move their relationship forward.

Then, out of nowhere, a senior one year above Jack confesses that she likes him. She is also Wesley's first love. Jack has no feelings for her, and out of loyalty to Wesley he can't accept. But she refuses to give up.

[Goal]

Emma's goal is to make Wesley jealous so he will finally notice her, while Jack's goal is to get the senior to stop chasing him.

[Turning Point]

After practice, Emma stops Jack in the empty gym.

Emma: "Pretend to be my boyfriend. Just until Wesley notices me."

Jack: "Why would I do that?"

Emma: "Because that senior won't leave you alone, right? We both win."

Jack hesitates, but with no other way out, he accepts. Their fake relationship begins.

[Central Question]

Will Emma win Wesley's heart, or will this pretend relationship start to feel a little too real?

[Subplots]

  1. The senior keeps chasing Jack.
  2. Jack and Wesley's friendship, and Jack's loyalty to Wesley (the senior is Wesley's first love).
  3. Wesley's slowly growing curiosity and feelings for Emma.
Act 2

Development

[Barrier]

Emma and Jack start their fake relationship, but their personalities clash and they bicker constantly.

Emma: "Can you at least pretend to care when we are together?"

Jack: "I am pretending. This is my absolute best."

[Action point]

Even so, Jack decides to pick out a small birthday present for Emma. While looking for something she might like, he realizes he has grown quite fond of her. He chooses a silver bracelet with a tiny basketball charm.

On Emma's birthday, they go on a small date. Wesley also comes by to wish Emma a happy birthday, but he sees Emma and Jack looking comfortable and happy together.

[Complication]

Wesley gets a strange feeling of jealousy and starts paying more attention to Emma. Emma thinks her plan is working wonderfully.

One day, the senior tells Emma to step away from Jack. Jack finds Emma looking quiet and down in the gym and checks on her. When he learns the senior is the reason, he warns the senior firmly.

Jack: "Stay away from her. I mean it."

After that, the senior stops bothering them. Emma realizes that her heart flutters around Jack now, and the two grow much closer.

[Reversal]

Meanwhile, Wesley realizes his feelings for Emma have changed and decides to confess. But before he can, he accidentally finds out that Emma and Jack's relationship started out fake. Wesley confronts Jack.

Wesley: "Is it true? Was it all pretend?"

Jack: "...It was my idea. I needed a way to keep the senior away."

Jack takes the blame to protect Emma. Wesley is also hurt to learn that Jack kept secrets from him. Wesley tells Jack he should step back from Emma.

[Barrier]

Jack has no excuse, so he suggests they stop the fake relationship. Although Emma and Jack are sad to let go, they hide their true feelings because they think they should return to their original places. Jack knows Emma used to like Wesley, so he keeps his growing feelings to himself, hoping she can be happy with Wesley.

Act 3

Resolution

After they stop, Wesley confesses his feelings to Emma, and she accepts. It is what she thought she wanted for so long, so she tries to smile and be happy.

Jack keeps his promise to himself. He still cares for Emma deeply, so he quietly supports her relationship with Wesley. He even mentions to Wesley what kind of snacks Emma likes and reminds him to take good care of her. Every day after practice, Jack stays alone in the gym shooting baskets to clear his head.

[Foreshadowing – character traits / objects]

But Emma can't quite forget Jack. Wesley is sweet and gentle, yet her mind keeps drifting back to Jack's blunt remarks, their playful bickering, and the silver bracelet he gave her, which she still wears. At games, her eyes naturally wander toward Jack instead of Wesley.

[Foreshadowing – actions / knowledge]

One rainy afternoon, Emma realizes she has forgotten her umbrella. At her locker, she finds one hanging there. It's Jack's.

She hears from a teammate that Jack walked to the gym in the rain without it. Standing by her locker, a sudden realization hits her as she remembers how accurately Jack always seemed to notice the little things about her—even things she never told him directly.

Emma leaves the umbrella behind, her heart racing, and runs to the gym through the soft pouring rain.

[Climax – answer to the Central Question]

She bursts into the gym, damp and out of breath. Jack is shooting baskets alone.

Jack: "Why aren't you using the umbrella?"

Without answering, Emma lets out all the thoughts she has been holding in.

Emma: "I can't stop thinking about you. Even when we bickered, my eyes kept going to you, and I..."

Before she can finish, Jack steps closer and pulls her into a soft, unexpected kiss.

Jack (quietly): "You really talk too much."

Jack: "...But you've been on my mind for a long time, too."

[Foreshadowing payoff – opening scene]

At the next game, Emma cheers brightly from the stands, just like before. But this time, she calls Jack's name. Jack, who once found her cheering noisy, looks up at her and gives a small, fond smile.

My Thoughts

Reflection

This was my first time writing a script, so it was quite difficult at first. I had to think carefully about how each scene would flow and how the characters would speak. Until now, I had mostly done assignments on my own, but this time we created the story as a group. Sharing ideas and deciding on the plot together made the process much more fun, and discussing everything in English helped me improve my English as well.
