My Thoughts

Reflection

Working with my partner on a Vogue-style beauty feature was a new experience for me. Choosing natural K-makeup as our topic was exciting because it is something many young women around the world are curious about. The hardest part was writing in the elegant, descriptive tone of a fashion magazine while still giving readers practical tips. Designing the layout together, choosing pictures and fonts, and dividing the work taught me how much visuals shape the way an article is read.
