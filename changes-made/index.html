Making “Real or fake?” stand out

“I understand that the “Real or Fake” section has a different color because it is important. However, it blends in with the background, so it doesn't stand out enough. I think using a more eye-catching color would make it better.”

— Chae Young Kim, classmate

The “Real or fake?” card is the most important conclusion of my review, but it used a navy color that was almost the same as the background. I changed it to a bright gold gradient with dark navy text, added a glowing border, and placed a small “Key question” label next to the number.

Now the card is the first thing readers notice in the analysis section.

Movie Plot ↗

A bigger movie poster

“The movie poster looks really good, but it is a bit too small. I think it would look better if it were bigger.”

— Chae Young Kim, classmate

I enlarged the poster from 220px to 320px wide, which is about 45% bigger. On mobile, it grew from 170px to 240px.

The title, cast, and photos on the poster are now much easier to see, and the poster balances better with the character descriptions.

Article ↗

A real magazine spread

“It would be nice to have the two pages arranged side by side horizontally so that the article looks like a real magazine.”

— Chae Young Kim, classmate

Before, the two article pages were stacked vertically, so readers had to scroll a long way. I arranged them side by side as an open magazine spread, with a soft shadow in the middle that looks like the fold of a real magazine.

On phones, the pages still appear one after another so they stay easy to read.

04
Article ↗

Zoom in to read the text

“It would also be helpful to let readers zoom in to read the text more easily.”

— Chae Young Kim, classmate

Before, clicking a page simply opened the image file in a new tab. I built a zoom viewer inside the website: readers can click a page, zoom from 100% to 300%, drag to move around, switch pages with the arrows, and press Esc to close.

Now the small text in the article can be read comfortably without leaving the page.

Separating Reflection and AI Acknowledgement

“I really like how the overall design uses calm and elegant colors… Overall, I think it is nearly perfect.”

— Chae Young Kim, classmate · Improved based on my own review

My classmate said this page was nearly perfect, so I reviewed it myself. The Reflection and AI Acknowledgement used the same beige background as the rest of the page, so they looked like part of the cover letter.

I placed each of them in its own navy card with gold headings. Now these two sections are clearly separated and easy to find.
